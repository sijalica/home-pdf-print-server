HOME LAB · PDF PRINTING

Print a document

Send one PDF to the household printer. One copy of every page will print.

This is a self-reported name, not authentication. Anyone could enter another person’s name. A later version can identify the uploader through a signed-in account and add multi-factor authentication (MFA).

1–100 pages. Total upload request: up to 20 MiB. Encrypted PDFs are not supported.

Completed uploads are deleted after CUPS reports completion, normally within about 15 seconds. Print history remains. Access is through the SSH tunnel.
